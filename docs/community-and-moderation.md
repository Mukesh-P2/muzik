# Muzik community and moderation rules (release draft)

> **Release blocker:** Replace `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`, establish who reviews reports,
> define a response process, and publish these rules before distribution.

Muzik rooms are shared spaces. Participants must not use display names, search,
queues, or playback to promote illegal content, threats, harassment, hate,
sexual exploitation, graphic abuse, impersonation, privacy violations, spam,
malware, fraud, or infringement of others' rights.

## Room-level controls

- Hosts can remove queued items and control playback.
- Hosts can delete room-chat messages and mute or unmute participants in chat.
- Queue and pause votes let participants influence room activity.
- Participants can leave a room at any time.
- Room codes should be shared only with intended participants.

Muzik currently has no public room directory, direct messaging, user accounts,
or durable content database. Chat retains at most 100 messages in the in-memory
room. Room-level controls are not a substitute for an operator abuse-response
process.

## Reporting

Report suspected abuse to `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`. Include the room code, approximate
UTC time, what happened, and any screenshots you can safely provide. Never send
member authentication tokens, passwords, government identifiers, or unrelated
private information.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Reports of
child sexual exploitation should also be made to the appropriate national
reporting authority.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, available evidence, and technical capability, responses
may include guidance to leave or recreate a room, removal of active room data,
rate limiting, blocking abusive traffic, preserving information when legally
required, or referral to the relevant platform or authority. Good-faith reports
should be handled confidentially to the extent reasonably possible.
